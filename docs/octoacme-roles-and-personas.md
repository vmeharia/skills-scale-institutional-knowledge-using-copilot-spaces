# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Collaborate on test automation, discuss acceptance criteria validation, support smoke testing efforts
- **With Technical Lead/Architect**: Receive technical guidance on design decisions and best practices, participate in code reviews and architecture discussions
- **With Product Managers**: Implement features to meet acceptance criteria, discuss feasibility and trade-offs
- **With Business Analyst**: Clarify detailed requirements and acceptance criteria during sprint planning
- **With Scrum Master**: Participate in ceremonies, escalate blockers and impediments
- **With Design/UX Lead**: Implement UI specifications and collaborate on implementation details

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Business Analyst**: Work together to clarify and document detailed requirements before development begins
- **With Technical Lead/Architect**: Discuss feasibility, technical trade-offs, and integration challenges
- **With Design/UX Lead**: Define user needs and validate design solutions through user research
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Discuss quality standards, acceptance criteria validation, and release readiness
- **With Developers**: Collaborate on feature acceptance criteria and clarify product intent
- **With Project Manager**: Align on prioritization and milestone delivery

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Scrum Master/Agile Coach**: Partner to manage sprint execution, escalate blockers, and monitor team metrics
- **With Product Manager**: Align on prioritization and milestone delivery
- **With Technical Lead/Architect**: Identify technical risks and dependencies to inform project timelines
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Coordinate QA cycles and release scheduling
- **With Developers**: Communicate timelines and ensure visibility on project progress
- **With Business Analyst**: Ensure requirements are clear and documented before development

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing Leads own the quality assurance strategy and testing approach for the project. They define test plans, validate acceptance criteria, and ensure product quality before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and QA approach (unit, integration, end-to-end)
- Write and maintain test cases and acceptance criteria validation
- Collaborate with developers on test automation and CI/CD integration
- Perform manual testing for edge cases and user experience validation
- Track and triage quality issues; report quality metrics
- Own smoke testing before production deployment
- Participate in retrospectives to identify quality improvements

### Goals
- Achieve high test coverage and reliability
- Catch defects early to reduce production incidents
- Provide confidence in release readiness
- Enable fast feedback loops during development

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and definition of done reviews
- Daily standups for blockers and test status
- Acceptance criteria discussion with Product Managers and Developers
- Post-release smoke test reporting

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Collaborate on test automation, discuss test coverage, and coordinate end-to-end testing
- **With Product Manager**: Validate acceptance criteria and determine quality standards for release
- **With Technical Lead/Architect**: Understand technical implementation to inform testing strategy
- **With Project Manager**: Report quality metrics, coordinate release timelines, and escalate quality blockers
- **With Design/UX Lead**: Validate usability and user experience through testing
- **With Business Analyst**: Clarify acceptance criteria and use cases for comprehensive test coverage

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate team ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach the team on process adherence. They ensure the team operates effectively within the agreed sprint or iteration cadence.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Track and escalate blockers and dependencies
- Coach team on iterative delivery and process improvement
- Maintain backlog health and sprint metrics (velocity, burndown)
- Protect team from scope creep and external distractions
- Support the Project Manager in communications and risk tracking

### Goals
- Maximize team flow and delivery consistency
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Reduce cycle time from planning to release
- Enable predictable delivery

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Weekly retrospectives and action item tracking
- Risk and blocker escalation to PM and Product Lead
- Metrics and velocity reports

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Remove impediments, facilitate ceremonies, and coach on process adherence
- **With Project Manager**: Support delivery coordination, escalate team-level risks and blockers
- **With Product Manager**: Communicate sprint progress and velocity to inform prioritization
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Coordinate testing cycles and quality acceptance into sprint planning
- **With Technical Lead/Architect**: Address technical blockers and ensure architecture discussions happen efficiently
- **With Business Analyst**: Ensure requirements are clarified early to prevent rework

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical strategy, design decisions, and risk mitigation. They ensure scalability, maintainability, and alignment with system architecture.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers and ensure code quality standards
- Collaborate with Product Managers on feasibility and trade-offs
- Guide integration points and cross-system dependencies
- Review and validate testing and deployment strategies

### Goals
- Ensure technical excellence and system reliability
- Reduce technical debt and risk
- Accelerate delivery through smart design
- Build scalable, maintainable systems

### Typical Communication
- Technical design discussions and architecture reviews
- Sprint planning and estimation guidance
- Code review and mentoring
- Dependency and risk escalation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Provide technical guidance on design decisions, conduct code reviews, mentor on best practices
- **With Product Manager**: Discuss feasibility, technical trade-offs, and estimate scope impact
- **With Project Manager**: Identify technical risks, dependencies, and complexity that impact timelines
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Guide on test strategy for complex technical scenarios and system integration
- **With Scrum Master**: Address technical blockers and ensure design discussions fit into sprint cadence
- **With Business Analyst**: Clarify technical requirements and integration dependencies

---

## Design / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design and UX Leads translate user needs into intuitive, usable interfaces. They own user research, design specifications, and usability validation.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and define user personas and journeys
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Validate design with users and stakeholders
- Collaborate with developers on implementation and accessibility
- Define design system and component standards
- Perform usability testing and gather user feedback

### Goals
- Maximize user satisfaction and adoption
- Reduce support burden through clear, intuitive design
- Maintain design consistency across products
- Inform product decisions with user insights

### Typical Communication
- Product backlog and acceptance criteria refinement
- Design reviews and stakeholder feedback
- User research and testing insights
- Developer collaboration on implementation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Provide design specifications, collaborate on implementation feasibility, review UI in development
- **With Product Manager**: Share user research insights, validate product direction, inform roadmap with usability findings
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Support usability testing and validate design through manual testing
- **With Project Manager**: Communicate design milestones and user research timelines
- **With Technical Lead/Architect**: Discuss technical feasibility of design solutions and accessibility requirements
- **With Business Analyst**: Ensure design aligns with business requirements and user workflows

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge product and engineering by clarifying requirements, documenting use cases, and ensuring solutions align with business needs.

### Responsibilities
- Work with Product Managers to clarify and document requirements
- Write detailed use cases and acceptance criteria
- Map business processes and identify dependencies
- Support estimation by providing detailed specifications
- Collaborate with QA to validate acceptance criteria
- Document decisions and trade-offs

### Goals
- Ensure requirements are clear and unambiguous
- Reduce rework due to misaligned expectations
- Accelerate development by providing detailed specs
- Maintain traceability from business needs to implementation

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and clarification sessions
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement
- Developer and QA collaboration on acceptance criteria
- Stakeholder updates on scope and progress

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Product Manager**: Work together to translate business vision into detailed requirements and acceptance criteria
- **With Developers**: Clarify detailed requirements during planning, answer implementation questions
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Ensure acceptance criteria are testable and comprehensive
- **With Technical Lead/Architect**: Map business processes to technical solutions and identify integration dependencies
- **With Project Manager**: Help scope projects by clearly defining requirements and dependencies
- **With Scrum Master**: Participate in sprint planning to ensure requirements are ready before development begins
